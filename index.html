THE SOLAR SYSTEM

The solar system is made up of sun and all the object that move around it. The sun is at the center and provides light and heat.
There are eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.
Other objects in the solar system include Moons, Asteroids, Comets, and Dwarf planets. The planets move around the sun in paths called ORBITS.
